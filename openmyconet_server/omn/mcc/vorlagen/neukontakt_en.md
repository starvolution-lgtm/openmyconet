**What OpenMycoNet does**

OpenMycoNet is an independent citizen science project. We study bioelectrical activity in fungal and mycorrhizal networks – in the field, at many distributed sites, using BioComm, a sensor platform we developed ourselves. Our guiding principle is: measurement data first, everything else afterwards. We publish the measurement data we collect under the CC BY 4.0 licence, while private or personal data stay local with the participants.

We deliberately work without a predetermined outcome. We distance ourselves from popular interpretations such as the "Wood Wide Web" narrative: the first task is to obtain reliable data, before any conclusions are drawn from it.

**Why the project came into being**

Electrophysiological studies on fungi have so far mostly been carried out in the laboratory. However, field data and laboratory data are not the same thing, and long-term field measurements at many locations are rare. In addition, funding logic tends to favour what is easy to apply for and to exploit commercially ("fundability bias", cf. Lehmann & Rillig 2025). We therefore regard independence as a method: those who collect data openly and without pressure to exploit them can ask questions that would otherwise remain unanswered. Our motto: "Citizen science from below".

**How to get involved**

- Mycelists (registered members) follow the project and its data.
- Hyphists (cooperation partners) work with us on a professional or practical basis; there is a dedicated collaboration area for this.
- Sporists (sponsors) support the project financially.

Anyone who contributes professionally does so explicitly with their own expertise – we do not see ourselves as a supplier of devices, but as an open platform for joint measurements.
